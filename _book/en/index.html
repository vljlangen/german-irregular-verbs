Welcome

Practise common German irregular verbs for free. Choose an exercise and a group of 10 verbs.

  • Principal parts: review the four key verb forms.

  • Conjugation: present, simple past and perfect.

  • Complete the sentences: preesens, imperfekti ja perfekti.

  • Write the whole sentence: preesens, imperfekti ja perfekti.

Check forms and meanings in the verb reference. Modal verbs have their own conjugation tables. There is also a separate conjugation table for sein.
