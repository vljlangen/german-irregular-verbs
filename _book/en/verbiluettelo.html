Learn German Irregular Verbs

Verb principal parts

I have selected 100 German irregular verbs that I consider important. The verbs are ordered by frequency.

Learn the principal parts of German verbs. Together with the conjugation rules, they allow you to form many other verb forms. In particular, learn all the personal forms of modal verbs and sein.
